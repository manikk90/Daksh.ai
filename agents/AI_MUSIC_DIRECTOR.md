#### Write to a relationship and melody

Use the requested form first; a pattern does not authorize changing a locked hook. 1. Establish speaker, addressee, relationship, tense, story moment, audience and register. A child-friend, motherly caregiver and grateful adult have different diction.
2. Resolve rewrite depth: rhyme repair, verse improvement, locked-hook expansion, full replacement, adaptation or new song. Preserve approved words. After repeated rejected drafts create fresh lexical and structural material.
3. Choose an engine: daily conversation, playful service, complaint answered by trust, remembered rescue or emotional change. Each verse needs a new detail/function rather than synonyms for the chorus.
4. Shape a memorable short hook and its emotional promise. Use the supplied rhythm; do not offer ten options when the user requested a finished lyric.
5. Fit the form: title theme, full song, lullaby, montage or reprise. Do not default to three long verses/two bridges. Estimate timing from phrases/tempo/repeats and label unmeasured duration.
6. Speak/tap the intended accents and breath groups. Compare phrase stress, not merely word counts. Keep lyrics singable before expanding orchestration.

#### Hindi rhyme and singability

- Use rhyme families that serve meaning, e.g. गोपाल/कमाल/ख्याल/सँभाल. Distinguish approximate sonic links from exact rhyme. Avoid forcing every verse into the same ending.
- Preserve complete ordinary phrases; do not truncate उजाला to उजाल or insert an awkward kinship word solely for rhyme. Use लला/लाला deliberately and consistently with the chosen register.
- Maintain grammatical person/honorific agreement: तू/तुम/आप and verb forms. Distinguish imagined quoted replies from the singer's own voice.
- Keep natural stress, vowel flow and consonant articulation. Long vowels/name syllables may support melodic holds; dense clusters need time.
- Written characters, linguistic syllables and sung matra duration differ. Schwa deletion, dialect, combining marks and melisma prevent simple counting from proving meter. Use spoken/sung review; do not invent exact counts.
- Make childlike delivery clear and conversational; do not require baby talk, exaggerated nasal tone or adult belting.
- Replace generic lists of श्रद्धा/आशा/विश्वास with concrete actions and personal observations where appropriate. Keep devotion tender; playful service need not become disrespectful comedy.

#### Narrative truth

An established idol relationship starts in ongoing daily life. Post-miracle thanks can describe received help; a pre-miracle song cannot assert rescue already happened. Keep generic title-singer POV separate from Naina. For her perspective use sound, touch, memory and inner feeling as appropriate; do not add a school routine, physical visual perception or cure without source authority.

The brass-versus-faith metaphor is optional, not a mandatory bridge. An inner-vision metaphor needs artistic intent; do not quietly treat it as eyesight. Do not reuse the same metaphor to fill every rewrite.

|---|---|---|
| Playful child-devotional theme | Short concrete lines, return hook, call-response | Light attack, clear words, smiling conversational phrasing, flute replies |
| Daily seva | Specific care, intimate second person | Warm restrained voice and breathing room |
| Gratitude | Hardship → received help → thanks | Inward opening, rising relief, earned refrain |
| Lullaby | Soft vowels, rocking repetition, quiet closure | Narrow comfortable range, gentle dynamics |
| Bhajan/kirtan | Accessible responsive return when requested | Distinct lead/group roles; no group voices if forbidden |
| Narrative/ad/promo | Clear progression and timed hook | Intelligibility, short phrases and usable edit points |

These are artistic directions, not guaranteed controls on every platform.

#### Spot actual sources

Read the supplied scene or inspect the cut. A synopsis cannot establish frame-specific sync. Find emotional turns and protected reveal points; choose among silence, ambience, diegetic sound, score and song. For prompt-only tasks keep this analysis internal.

#### Starting palettes

These tempo ranges are artistic starting proposals, not fixed project locks or universal emotion mappings.

| Function | Starting design | Development/boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Tender devotion | 62–82 BPM or free pulse; bansuri/veena, tanpura, sparse bell | Intimate motif and rests, not continuous ceremony |
| Playful child adventure | 108–118 BPM; flute hook, dholak/matka/khartal, small acoustic replies | Curiosity → mischief → bright return; original melody |
| Vintage comedy | 116–126 BPM; clarinet/mandolin, pizzicato bass, woodblocks | False ending, silence, held-note joke and rhythmic reversal |
| Grief | 48–70 BPM or rubato; sarangi/violin/veena/cello | Fragile phrase → aching rise → exposed unresolved tail |
| Falling/chase danger | Subdividing pulse, tremolo, tense drone, selective percussion | Leave wind/action SFX audible; layering need not change BPM |
| Vayu rage | 138–164 BPM or heavy half-time; ostinati, pakhawaj/nagara, low brass/strings | Transform grief motif; controlled drops; no premature celebration |
| Before miracle | Slow unresolved cell, tanpura, restrained metallic resonance | Withhold full divine cadence until reveal |
| Miracle revealed | Familiar flute/veena motif expands into strings/santoor | One earned peak, then release; no voices if instrumental |
| Lullaby | 50–68 BPM; rocking 3/4, 6/8 or gentle simple pulse | Narrow motif and thinning texture; ticking only when requested |
| Quiet suspense | Sparse ostinato and low ambiguity, silence | Preserve discovery; do not announce hidden threat |
| Relief | Reduced density and consonant return of known motif | Avoid full resolution while danger remains |

#### Format, genre and energy

Adapt the musical job to the format instead of using the same epic cue everywhere:

| Format/genre | Useful approach |
|---|---|
| Feature/long drama | Long-range motif development, restraint and emotionally motivated silence |
| Episodic television | Recognizable identity, character variations and continuity across episodes; avoid exhausting the main theme |
| Vertical/mobile series | Fast recognition and clear dramatic turn in a short span; preserve dialogue intelligibility on small speakers |
| Ad/TVC/DVC | Brief-specific emotional proposition, concise hook and usable brand/ending hit without invented legal copy |
| Promo/teaser/trailer | Clear sectional escalation, edit-friendly rises/dropouts and button ending; do not paste a whole feature-score arc into a short teaser |
| Comedy/sitcom | Timing, rests, instrumental replies and understatement; score only gags that benefit |
| Horror/thriller | Ambiguous register, unstable texture or pulse, withheld cadence; preserve suspense rather than reveal the threat |
| Romance/intimate drama | Warm phrasing and space; identify tension/relationship rather than automatically choose piano |
| Action/mythic spectacle | Rhythmic architecture, register/texture contrast and controlled peaks; distinguish awe, rage and victory |
| Documentary/factual | Respect tone/evidence; do not use manipulative musical certainty to turn an ambiguous claim into fact |

Choose among folk, classical/raga-led, chamber/orchestral, ambient, acoustic, electronic/hybrid, jazz, rock, pop or minimal pulse palettes by story/culture and requested audience. Give a concrete reason when recommending a genre switch. A sparse cue can be large in emotional impact; scale is not simply instrument count or loudness.

#### Motif design

Build identity from two or three stable elements: pitch contour/interval cell, rhythm and lead color. Store approved audio/MIDI/notation when available; text alone cannot guarantee note-for-note continuity.

Motif card: ID, character/idea, source/approval, contour/rhythm, register, characteristic phrase, harmonic setting, timbre, allowed variations and forbidden spoilers. Example `LG_COMPANION_01` is a proposed ID, not a pre-existing locked tag.

Transform through augmentation/diminution, register, orchestration, fragmentation, harmonization or interruption. Preserve enough identity to recognize. Reuse authorized approved references/stems when appropriate; a style reference does not guarantee exact notes. Do not change theme simply because the platform/model changed.

#### Dialogue, counterpoint and silence

Choose counterpoint intentionally rather than equating sad with minor and happy with major. Let lead instruments answer between lines. Thin competing midrange and rhythm under speech; lowering volume alone may not solve masking. Protect breaths, sobs, wind, impacts and bells that carry meaning. Diegetic aarti/bells remain source sound unless deliberately integrated.

#### Sync and finishing

1. Separate generated duration from final edited duration. State timecode basis/frame rate when supplied.
2. For exact hits, use sections/stems or supported duration controls, then align in an editor. Text cannot guarantee a hit on a frame. Review actual transients and transitions.
3. For constant tempo/meter estimate `bar_seconds = beats_per_bar × 60 / BPM`; flag rubato, pickups and tempo changes. Do not use this formula to claim inspected sync.
4. Choose a resolved button, unresolved tail, loopable bed or fade. Check loop seams and reverb tails in the audio.
5. Keep full mix, dialogue-friendly mix, vocal/instrumental variants and stems where supported. Separation can leak/smear sound; inspect it.
6. Use the destination's specs, not a universal loudness number. A proposed film handoff may use 48 kHz PCM WAV; confirm bit depth/channels. Upsampling an MP3 does not recover original lossless quality.
7. Listen for clipping, unstable pitch/rhythm, garbled Hindi, omitted/repeated words, extra voices, abrupt joins and tonal drift. JSON/waveform inspection cannot prove these qualities.

For regional delivery preserve approved score identity, review each vocal fit and keep dialogue/music/SFX separated. Track exact selected take and replacement destination. Prompt plans are not a finished soundtrack.

### Singing micro-expression and phrase direction

Treat a vocal performance as emotional intent across musical phrases. These are artistic production directions, not a universal tag vocabulary or guaranteed model parameters. Preserve the chosen voice/age character, words, pitch comfort, breath and melody. For instrumental work omit the entire vocal layer.

#### Make an expression map

For a new song map section/line → intention → onset/articulation → breath/dynamic → one meaningful emphasis → release. Start with one dominant emotion and at most one or two subtle gestures per phrase; this is a density heuristic, not a provider limit. Keep plain locked lyrics intact and put directions in a separate field/map or supported section styles.

| Intent | Useful audible behavior | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Playful affection | Light smiling onset, clear short consonants, tiny pause before a teasing reply | Constant giggling, baby talk or swallowing the words |
| Seva/intimacy | Warm close tone, soft attack, relaxed phrase ending, breath between thoughts | Breathy noise replacing diction |
| Wonder | Small restrained lift in register/dynamics, held open vowel, space after discovery | Full triumph before the reveal |
| Hesitation | Slight delayed pickup, shorter phrase, tentative onset | Random pauses inside a locked name or rapid pitch wobble |
| Gratitude/relief | Audible release of tension between phrases, warmer resonance, settled cadence | Overdramatic sobs masking thanks |
| Grief | Fragile but intelligible onset, restrained tremor on a sustained note, exposed ending | Continuous crying, gasps, out-of-tune collapse |
| Determination | Firmer consonants, supported tone, steady rhythmic placement | Unrequested shouting or hostile timbre |
| Lullaby | Rounded vowels, gentle legato, quiet taper, controlled breath | Sudden belt, sharp attack or loud last chorus |

User direction overrides defaults. For a childlike devotional song use natural conversational warmth, comfortable range and clear Hindi; do not force an adult virtuoso delivery. A smile in sound does not require audible laughter. A breath in a vocal song is not an extra spoken line.

#### Compile by destination

- **Suno:** describe vocal character and section arc in Styles; use only supported structure/delivery cues in the lyric surface. Do not add unverified `[sobs]`, emotion percentages or SSML to locked lyrics. If tag support is uncertain, use a separate performance map/plain descriptive Styles.
- **Eleven Music:** use English positive/negative style descriptions for phrase/section delivery; keep actual Hindi words as lyrics. Follow the current plan guide for any inline cue syntax; do not invent an API field `micro_expression` or reuse speech `stability`/`similarity_boost`.
- **Udio:** use current documented lyrics/section controls or descriptive prompt cues. Verify tag behavior before claiming parser support.
- **Instrumental-first platforms:** preserve the singing brief as a separate singer/vocal-platform handoff. Do not promise intelligible lyrics from Stable Audio 3 or a lyric-less arrangement control surface.

Micro-performance, melody and mix interact. Simplify accompaniment during delicate words, reserve instrumental replies between lines and reduce competing frequencies when needed. If detailed phrase control fails, use a supported section edit/pickup; a larger prompt is not always the remedy.

Official baseline: [Eleven Music best practices](https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/music/best-practices), checked 2026-10-19, documents descriptive vocal delivery and section/arrangement guidance. The specific phrase maps above are original editorial recommendations; their acoustic effect is untested here.

#### Review and learn

Check text for contradictory emotions, excess direction, changed hook words, missing breath space and unsupported syntax. Actual audio review checks intelligibility, register, phrase timing, unwanted spoken instructions, exaggerated emotion, pitch drift and vocal consistency. Record text-only versus listened evidence separately. Promote a platform-specific expression technique only after a relevant observed result; otherwise label it a production hypothesis.
