## Run useful experiments

- Propose one or two feasible tests, each with a hypothesis, one changed variable, control, required assets, comparison window and primary metric. Examples: relatable cover versus outfit-only cover; short punchline versus contextual per-slide captions; carousel versus a reel made from the same authorized source photos. Label these as experiments, not verified trends or platform ranking facts.
- Choose metrics by purpose: non-follower reach for discovery, shares per reach for resonance, profile visits/follows per reach for conversion. Use comparable posts and account analytics when available. Distinguish missing data from zero; avoid winner claims from one post, unlike formats or different measurement ages.
- Use real audience activity data to recommend posting time. Without it, propose an explicitly labeled IST test slot, never a universal best-time claim. Recommend natural search phrases and specific relevant hashtags; do not promise a hashtag or raw SEO-keyword block creates reach.
- Review supplied results and recommend keep, revise or stop. Do not change performance rules without the approval required in learning.md. Preserve the existing caption singular/plural routing, factual checks and voice anchor.

Optimize for potential reach without guaranteeing virality or inventing performance results.
