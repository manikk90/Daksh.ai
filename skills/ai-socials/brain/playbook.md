Machine rules live in `playbook_rules.json` (versioned by the weekly retro).
Hard limits live in `platform_rules.json` -> `hard_constraints`. This file is the
reasoning behind them, written for the Social Media Manager agent. When a learned
rule in `playbook_rules.json` contradicts something here, the learned rule wins
because it is backed by Manik's own numbers.

## Repurposing chain (one idea, four native posts)

1. Make the core piece once: a "Jai Hanuman Lab" breakdown of one episode.
2. LinkedIn: 8-12 slide carousel, the how and the why.
3. Instagram: 20-45s Reel, result first, fast breakdown, AI label on.
4. Facebook: Hindi voice-over cut of the same Reel, uploaded natively.
5. X: 5-tweet thread with the clip in tweet 1; links only in your own reply.

Never upload STAGE's official promo unchanged. Re-edit and add your making-of
layer, otherwise Meta's originality rules bury it.

## Platform notes

**LinkedIn.** Optimise for saves and sends, then comments with substance.
Hook line has to earn "see more". No outbound link in the body. 0-3 specific
hashtags (#AIFilmmaking #RegionalOTT), never #GrowthMindset-type tags. Reply to
every comment in the first 90 minutes.

**Instagram.** First 3 seconds decide skip rate. Open on the finished shot.
Write captions with search keywords (AI animation, Haryanvi, Hanuman) because
captions are indexed; hashtags (max 5) only categorise. Use Trial Reels to test
hooks on non-followers. Turn the AI label on for AI media.

**Facebook.** Turn on Professional Mode (needed for any stats). Hindi or
Hinglish. Devotional and making-of clips. Native upload only.

**X.** Replies are the growth engine: 5-10 thoughtful replies a day to global
AI-video builders and tool teams. Mutual-follow conversations get the biggest
boost. Clip in the post, link in your own reply.

## Engagement routine (20 minutes, daily)

5-8 comments on accounts in `targets.json`. A good comment adds a number, a
counter-example, or a detail from your pipeline. Never "Great post!". No
engagement pods.
