## Separate similarly named cloning controls

For Dubbing v2 language creation, the refinement guide documents `cloning_strength` from 0 to 10, default 7, in language `voice_settings`. Do not normalize it to the distinct voice-settings `clone_strength` 0–1 field recorded in earlier research. The language-creation call queues billable generation; do not call it merely to inspect settings. Editing and regeneration endpoints in the refinement guide require enterprise workspaces; public documentation is not account eligibility. Sources: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-api/guides/how-to/dubbing/refine-and-regenerate and https://elevenlabs.io/docs/api-reference/dubbing/language-targets/create-language-target . Publication dates unavailable.

Record project/language revisions and actual output revision before treating an edited transcript as rendered audio. Keep source edits distinct from target-language edits. Do not change approved words to fit a timing target.

## Pronunciation and pause controls

The current TTS guide documents selective slash-wrapped IPA for v4 and excludes SSML break tags for v3/v4. Exact pronunciation remains voice/phrase dependent. Keep pronunciation experiments in a separately versioned rendering copy; canonical dialogue/subtitles remain unchanged. Source: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/overview/capabilities/text-to-speech/best-practices .

The TTS dictionary guide restricts non-English phonemes to v4/v4 Turbo, while the Agents dictionary page also lists v3. Keep non-English v3 phoneme support unresolved and route-specific; do not promise Hindi IPA on v3 or infer dictionary support in Dubbing v2. Alias dictionaries are a documented alternative, not verified acoustic fidelity. Sources: https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-api/guides/how-to/text-to-speech/pronunciation-dictionaries and https://elevenlabs.io/docs/eleven-agents/customization/voice/pronunciation-dictionary .
